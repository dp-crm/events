Things to look at

    How this was worked out

    FIRE number = yearly spending at retirement ÷ safe withdrawal rate. Spending today (after removing EMIs that end, and any pension or rent) is grown by inflation to your retirement age.

    Earliest age = the first age at which your investments, growing with your monthly additions, reach the number needed at that age.

    Where the assumptions come from

    • Retail inflation (CPI): 6.09% in October 2026 (MoSPI).
    • Safe withdrawal rate for India: research puts it near 3% (Saraogi and Raju); planners use 3 to 3.5%. Lower for longer retirements.
    • Equity returns: Nifty 50 Total Return Index has delivered about 12% a year over 10 to 20 years. We use less, as a margin.
    • Debt: 10-year government bond yield about 7.1 to 7.5% (RBI).
    • Medical costs: rising 11.5 to 14% a year, so a share of your spending is inflated faster.
    • Life expectancy at 60: about 17.5 more years for men and 19.8 for women (SRS 2020-24), so we plan well beyond that.

    Markets do not give fixed returns. This is a planning estimate, to be reviewed every year.
